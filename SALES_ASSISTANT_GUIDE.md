# Sales Assistant AI - Agent Support Guide

## Overview
The **Sales Assistant** is an intelligent chatbot that helps sales agents with real-time sales support, customer communication, commission calculations, and sales coaching.

Available 24/7 at the bottom-right of the agent portal! 🤖

---

## 🎯 What the Sales Assistant Can Help With

### 1. **Sales Tips** 💡
Proven sales techniques including:
- Building rapport with customers
- Presenting benefits vs features
- 3-tier sales approach (budget/popular/premium)
- Handling objections positively
- Closing techniques
- Bonus strategies based on local markets

**When to use:**
- You're starting your day
- New to a sales area
- Want to improve conversion rate
- Need confidence boost

---

- **Opening statement** (First 30 seconds)

- **Your rate:** 12% of every sale

- **Monthly target:** KES 500,000 = KES 60,000 commission

**When to use:**
- Before showing deal to customer
- Calculating your earnings
- Planning monthly targets
- Motivating yourself

## 🎮 How to Use the Sales Assistant

### Opening the Chat
1. Look for the **blue chat bubble** at bottom-right of your screen
2. Click it to open the Sales Assistant
3. Start with quick topics or type your own question

### Quick Reply Buttons
Available topics at the top:
- Sales tips
- Customer scripts
- Commission calc
- Lead strategies
- Financing terms
- Handling objections
- Motivation

Just click any button to get instant help!

### Typing Your Own Questions
Not limited to quick buttons! You can ask:
- "How do I explain the 12-month plan?"
- "What's a good way to find leads in my area?"
- "I lost a sale, help me!"
- "What commission do I earn for KES 75,000 sale?"

### Closing the Chat
Click the **X** button in top-right to close. Your conversation is saved!
